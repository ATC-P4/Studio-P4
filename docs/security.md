# Safety Recommendations

In order to avoid the risks associated with using Studio P4 with the recommended Akai MPK Mini Plus keyboard, here are the Safety Recommendations:



### Indoor use only. 
    Do not use the device in rain, near water, or in very humid conditions. Store the keyboard and laptop in a dry place.
### Keep liquids away. 
    Do not place drinks or other liquids near the keyboard or laptop.
### Use while stationary. 
    Operate the keyboard only while seated or stationary.
### Disconnect before moving. 
    Unplug the MIDI keyboard before relocating to avoid pulling devices off surfaces.
### Use recommended cables. 
    Use appropriately long cables with strain relief; avoid stretched or taut cables.
### Use clean, dry hands. 
    Ensure hands are clean and dry before using the device to prevent contamination and hardware wear.
### Keep system updated. 
    The application runs offline—keep your operating system and dependencies up to date.
