# Saving & Loading Projects

Studio P4 automatically handles file management for you. You do not need to use a mouse or keyboard to name files or find folders. Everything is handled directly through your MIDI controller and the Voice Assistant.

## 1. Saving Your Work

You can save your project at any time while in **Track Mode**. 

The studio uses a "Double-Tap" safety feature to ensure you don't accidentally save over something or trigger the save sequence by mistake.

1. Using your joystick, push **UP** repeatedly until you reach the very top of your track list (usually the Master track). 
2. Push **UP** one more time. The voice assistant will warn you: *"Appuyez à nouveau vers le haut pour sauvegarder"* (Press up again to save).
3. If you want to cancel, simply push Down or Left.
4. To confirm the save, push **UP** one final time. 
5. The voice will announce: *"Projet sauvegardé"*.

*Note: The studio automatically creates a folder with the current date and time on your computer, saving both the MIDI data and your instrument choices.*

## 2. Loading an Existing Project

Loading a project is done the moment you launch the application. Before the main studio loads, you will hear the voice say: *"Menu de démarrage. Nouveau projet."* (Startup Menu. New Project).

1. Push your joystick **DOWN** to browse your previously saved projects. The voice will read the dates and times of your past sessions.
2. Push your joystick **UP** to go back up the list.
3. Once you hear the name of the project you want to load, push the joystick **RIGHT** to confirm your selection.
4. The voice will say *"Chargement du projet"*, and you will be brought into the studio with all your tracks, loops, and instruments exactly as you left them!
