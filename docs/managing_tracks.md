# Managing Tracks & Instruments

Studio P4 uses a highly tactile, joystick-driven navigation system. 

You can control your entire studio using just the main joystick on your AKAI keyboard. 

The joystick operates in three different "Modes", allowing you to navigate your studio in layers. 
To switch from one mode to another, we use the left-right movement, while the up and down movement is used to interact with the mode. 

## 1. Track Mode (Navigating your project)

When you start the studio, your joystick is automatically in **Track Mode** (*Mode Piste*). In this mode, the joystick moves you up and down your list of tracks.

* **Select a Track (Up/Down):** Push the joystick **UP** or **DOWN** to change which track is currently "selected" (the track that receives your keyboard notes). The voice will announce the name of the newly selected track.
* **Mute a Track (Left):** Push the joystick **LEFT** to mute or unmute the currently selected track. The voice will announce *"muté"* or *"démuté"*.

### Creating a New Track
To add a new instrument to your song, you need a new track. 

1. Push the joystick **DOWN** until you reach the bottom of your track list. The voice will say: *"Appuyez vers le bas à nouveau pour ajouter une piste."*
2. Push the joystick **DOWN** one more time (Double-tap). 
3. The studio will instantly create a new track, select it, and the voice will announce *"Nouvelle piste ajoutée"*.

## 2. Group Mode (Changing Instrument Folders)

If you want to change the sound of your track (for example, switching from a Piano to a Drum Kit), push the joystick **RIGHT** once. 

The voice will say *"Mode groupe"*. You are now browsing the folders inside your SoundFont library.

* Push **UP** or **DOWN** to cycle through your instrument groups (e.g., Synths, Guitars, Drums). The voice will announce the folder name and automatically load the first instrument inside it.

## 3. Instrument Mode (Changing the Specific Sound)

Once you have found the right group, you can pick the exact sound you want. Push the joystick **RIGHT** one more time.

The voice will say *"Mode instrument"*. You are now browsing the individual `.sf2` files inside your selected group.

* Push **UP** or **DOWN** to cycle through the specific instruments (e.g., Electric Piano, Grand Piano, Honky Tonk). The instrument changes instantly so you can test it on your keyboard.

### Returning to Track Mode
When you are happy with your new sound, simply push the joystick **LEFT** twice to back out of the menus. The voice will announce *"Mode piste"*, and you are ready to record!
